Alfie VR Controls

Quest Touch controllers in the headset browser (https://<robot>:8443). Nothing moves until you move: each subsystem starts from the robot's measured pose.

LLeft controller

  1. 1 Thumbstick BaseView

    Tilt up/down drives forward/back, left/right strafes. Up to 0.15 m/s.

    Click toggles passthrough. You see the room; the robot's head freezes where it is.

  2. 2 Y button Arms

    Moves both arms to zero.

  3. 3 X button holdView

    Shows the controls key in the headset in place of the HUD. Let go to return.

  4. 4 Trigger index fingerGripper

    Left gripper, proportional: released = open, full pull = closed. Only while the grip is held.

  5. 5 Grip hold, middle fingerArm

    Left arm follows the controller. Release to freeze the arm; grab again to carry on from there.

  6. 6 Menu button

    Not used.

RRight controller

  1. 1 Thumbstick Base● Record

    Tilt left/right turns in place. Up to 0.5 rad/s. Up/down does nothing.

    Click starts or stops episode recording.

  2. 2 B button Back

    Raises the back to the next preset.

  3. 3 A button Back

    Lowers the back to the next preset.

  4. 4 Trigger index fingerGripper

    Right gripper, proportional: released = open, full pull = closed. Only while the grip is held.

  5. 5 Grip hold, middle fingerArm

    Right arm follows the controller. Release to freeze the arm; grab again to carry on from there.

  6. 6 Meta button

    Headset system button. Not used by Alfie.

If things go wrong

  • Let go of the grip to stop an arm instantly.
  • Headset or controller lost: the base stops within 0.3 s; arms and back hold position.
  • E-stop: VR input is ignored and everything holds the measured pose.
